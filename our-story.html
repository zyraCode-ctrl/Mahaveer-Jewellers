Our Story

Since 2008, Built on Trust.

In 2008, Himmatbhai Sutariya founded Mahaveer Jewellers in the Baroda area of Surat. He built it on Pramanikata, honesty, and on a simple habit that has never changed: every customer who walks in is treated like family.

The Foundation

Himmatbhai Sutariya

Founder · Since 2008

Himmatbhai founded Mahaveer Jewellers in 2008, and he remains the main backbone and primary support of the business today.

The trust customers place in Mahaveer comes from him: from his honesty, the way he deals with every customer, and the way he has trained our staff to do the same. It is why relationships that began in 2008 are still going today.
